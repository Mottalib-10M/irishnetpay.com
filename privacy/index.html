Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

irishnetpay.com is published by Radif Partners. This policy explains, under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Ireland’s Data Protection Act 2018, what happens to information about your visit. Put simply: the site sets no cookies and the publisher stores no personal data about you.

Figures you enter

Gross salary, pay frequency, tax credits, marital status, PRSI class, pension contributions or welfare details: everything you type to work out take-home pay, income tax, USC, PRSI or a social welfare payment is calculated by JavaScript on your own device. It is not sent to any server, not written to a database and not saved on your device either; closing the tab clears it.

Cookies

Neither the calculators nor the stability tool described below set any cookie, and nothing is written to your browser’s local or session storage. That is why no cookie banner appears: there is nothing to accept or refuse. The site carries no advertising and no advertising trackers.

Anonymous stability monitoring

To find what goes wrong (JavaScript errors, slow loading, buttons that do nothing when clicked, broken scrolling), the site uses Microsoft Clarity in cookieless mode. In this mode Clarity sets none of _clck, _clsk, MUID or CLID; each page view has its own identifier, and separate page views or visits are never linked. Page text and every value typed into a field are masked in your browser before anything is sent, so Microsoft never sees your salary or your tax credits.

  • Controller: Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited, One Microsoft Place, South County Business Park, Leopardstown, Dublin 18, which may process the data in the United States under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses (Microsoft’s privacy statement).
  • Purpose: a technical, anonymous check that the pay and welfare calculators load and give results.
  • Retention: 30 days for page-view recordings, 13 months for aggregated figures.
  • Legal basis: the publisher’s legitimate interest in running calculators that work (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Objecting: block clarity.ms in your browser or content blocker (the calculators work exactly the same), or email contact@irishnetpay.com.

Hosting logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), whose servers log technical request data (IP address, time, page requested, browser) for security and to keep the service running. The publisher has no access to these logs and does not use them.

Emails

If you write to contact@irishnetpay.com, your message is used to reply and for nothing else; your address is not added to any list or shared.

Your rights

The GDPR gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection. As the publisher keeps no data about you, in practice they apply to any email you have sent, and, for stability monitoring, can also be exercised with Microsoft. You may complain to the Data Protection Commission, 6 Pembroke Row, Dublin 2, D02 X963, Ireland (how to contact the DPC), or to the CNIL in France, where the publisher is established.
